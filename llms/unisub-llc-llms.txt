# UniSub (Unisub LLC)

> UniSub is a recurring crypto-payments platform that lets merchants accept
> automated on-chain subscriptions and deposits. Customers pay directly from
> their own wallets (MetaMask, WalletConnect, Coinbase Wallet) in USDT, USDC,
> DAI, WBTC, WETH and other ERC20/Polygon tokens across EVM-compatible chains
> (Ethereum, BSC, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism). Subscriptions are executed by
> smart contracts; merchants integrate via an embeddable payment widget,
> checkout links, or the platform's API/webhooks, and manage subscribers in a
> hosted dashboard. UniSub is a 500 Global portfolio company based in Batumi,
> Georgia. A second product, WalletPulse, targets wallet analytics for iGaming.
